# Small tasks for the venue directory

Help a researcher find a plausible publication route or check one piece of evidence. A browser and a short note are enough; no code, installation or paid tool is required.

| Task | Suggested first attempt | What you return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Try a shortlist for one research topic](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/5) | 10–15 minutes | Your field, shortlist or no-fit result, and one useful observation. |
| [Check Research Ethics author costs](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/6) | 15 minutes | Submission and publication costs separately, with conditions, official sources and check date. |
| [Check JOSS AI disclosure](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/7) | 20 minutes | Covered AI uses, restrictions, what to disclose and where, with official links. |
| [Document International Relations editors](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/8) | 20 minutes | A current named editor and role, affiliation if stated, source and date. |
| [Trace a Journal of Physics D article](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/9) | 15–20 minutes | One article's title, DOI and exact journal identity, identifying metadata-only checks. |
| [Check a three-venue scope comparison](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/10) | 20–30 minutes | Three official scope/article-type sources and reasons each venue fits or does not. |

## Take a task

Open its issue and comment that you would like to help. Cole / @ajhcs is the human maintainer and confirms claims and acceptance. Claim one task at a time; we start with at most three active claims across the launch. The issue's current status and comments take precedence over this starting list.

Reply in the issue with the requested note, source links, date checked and any material AI assistance. Confirmation of an existing record can be useful. If a page is blocked or a policy is unclear, record that limit; do not guess. Maintainers prepare data changes after review.

Without a GitHub account, [email Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu?subject=OSRI%20venue%20directory%20task) with the task link and your note. We ask before creating a public record from an email. Public credit is optional; tell us your preferred name/handle or request no public credit. [Accepted work is recorded once per agreed task](README.md), and credit is separate from authorship.

## Make a small correction directly

You do not need to claim a task to [report a correction or source](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/new?template=venue-evidence.yml) or [share a directory-use report](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/new?template=directory-use.yml). A public topic description is enough; no manuscript upload is needed. We ask separately before quoting an experience publicly.

Try the [90-second directory walkthrough](../docs/venue-directory-demo.md) or read the [full contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
