# OSRI community forum

GitHub Discussions is enabled in the [public `.github` repository](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions). This guide describes the initial forum. The shared [OSRI hub](../README.md) links the research guides and project directory.

Q&A and Ideas have short forms. GitHub sign-in is required to post. The [publication receipt](../docs/review-notes.md) records the deployed files and checks.

## Find a conversation

| Category | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Q&A](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions/categories/q-a) | Ask a question, request a resource, or get help with research tools and methods. |
| [Ideas](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions/categories/ideas) | Propose a research question, method, project, or improvement. |
| [Show and tell](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/.github/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) | Share publications, models, useful work, and lessons. State what was checked and what remains uncertain. |
| General | Discuss collaboration and topics that do not fit another category. |
| Announcements | Read administrator updates and reply to them. |
| Polls | Gather community preferences. An informal poll does not establish a research finding. |

A short question is enough to start. You do not need a completed topic brief, prior literature search, code, or an agent. Follow the [contribution standards](contribution-standards.md). Cite evidence when you make a research claim, and mark a source lead as unchecked when needed.

You may use your agent to prepare a contribution within the authority you have given it. A human remains responsible for the submission. Do not describe automated review or an accepted forum answer as independent scientific review.

## Turn a conversation into work

When people agree on a task, a maintainer links or creates an issue in the relevant project repository. The issue states the task, responsible person, and checks. Pull requests hold proposed changes. An organization Project can provide an overall task view when needed.

Keep links between the conversation, task, and resulting research record. Record methods and conclusions in the project repository. Keep publication metadata in the shared register. A question may remain a useful conversation without becoming a project.

## Resource requests and private contact

Post public titles, identifiers, and research purposes in Q&A. Use the [resource request guide](resource-requests.md) for the next steps.

For confidential requests, contact [Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu). You can contact Cole directly without opening a public discussion. Use email to arrange permitted access, delivery, and controlled storage. Do not send credentials. Agree on the delivery method and storage before sending restricted files.

The initial administrator is [Cole / @ajhcs](https://github.com/ajhcs). A separate moderation panel has not been appointed. Ask for clarification or reconsideration when a decision is unclear. Administrators record relevant decisions and improvements through OSRI's [meta-research role](../meta-research/README.md).

GitHub can use a repository as the source for an organization-wide Discussions tab. That setting has not been configured here. Changing the source repository does not transfer existing discussions automatically. See [GitHub's organization Discussions guide](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-organization-settings/enabling-or-disabling-github-discussions-for-an-organization).
