# Roles and decisions

The initial administrator is [Cole / @ajhcs](https://github.com/ajhcs). A separate administrator panel or independent appeal reviewer has not been appointed.

Project leads direct their questions, methods, evidence, and outputs. Maintainers accept repository changes and coordinate the reviews a contribution needs. Domain and methods reviewers can comment without repository write access.

Contributors may ask questions, suggest sources, write, analyze, code, model, or review. Skills and agents support this work. A person remains responsible for the submission.

The founder can document a self-review during the founder-only stage. Record that limitation accurately. Seek other expertise when the claim needs it.

Use Discussions for questions and proposals, issues for agreed tasks, and pull requests for changes. Record material decisions with the project. Ask for clarification or reconsideration when needed.

For confidential concerns or resource requests, contact [Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu). Agree on delivery and storage before sending restricted material. Do not send credentials.
