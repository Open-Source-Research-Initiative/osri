# Free publication priority

The founder directed OSRI on November 16, 2026, toward research submitted to places that offer free submission and a chance of free publication.

OSRI's default shortlist will use credible venues with evidence of no mandatory author payment for the selected submission and publication route. Conditions on length, article type, membership, registration, and author eligibility remain attached to the record. Conditional waivers and funding agreements stay separate from the generally free list; unresolved costs stay unverified.

The [free publication guide](../../publications/free-publication.md) and [venue directory](../../publications/venues/README.md) implement this priority across the existing research areas. Human direction, the initial nonclinical healthcare scope, and the boundary against new physical laboratory experiments remain in place. Venue comparisons do not establish acceptance odds or select a project's target without the human lead's decision.

This is a publication priority, not a new version of OSRI's shared contribution standards. Public release of this implementation is separate from the founder's direction recorded here.
