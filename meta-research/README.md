# Research practice and meta-research

Meta-research is part of administering OSRI across its research areas and repositories. OSRI's administrators coordinate studies and improvements of the group's research practices.

Agentic-Powered-Research is human-driven: people define the research questions, direct the work, and evaluate the evidence and conclusions.

The administrative role includes:

- Examining and improving research methods, evidence quality, and reproducibility.
- Evaluating how Agentic-Powered-Research affects research quality, collaboration, time, and use of resources.
- Maintaining shared repository practices that connect questions, sources, methods, analyses, decisions, and findings.
- Coordinating publication records and reviewing what those records establish about the group's research activity.
- Sharing findings and improvements across projects and using them to improve how OSRI works.
- Maintaining the [skills and workflow catalog](../tooling/README.md), [resource catalog](../resources/README.md), and [topic-selection guidance](../topics/README.md).
- Reviewing changes to [open exchange formats](../governance/open-formats.md) and the [administrator resource request process](../governance/resource-requests.md).

This responsibility applies across the organization, including the shared Simulation Lab. Findings about OSRI's research practices can also become publications.

The [research areas and publication plan](../areas/README.md) describes the working research structure and publication approach.
