# Fisheries and policy with ViNoS

**Question:** How can a model connect fishing decisions, ecological conditions, and policy?

[Lemmen et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.05731) present ViNoS, a NetLogo model for small-scale fisheries. The [open JOSS article](https://joss.theoj.org/papers/10.21105/joss.05731) describes its purpose and links source and archived artifacts. This example broadens the catalog beyond financial and physical models into coupled social and ecological simulation.

**Access pending:** The model's source host returned an access challenge during this review. The [archive DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7928199) provides an alternative retrieval route. The model artifact and its license have not yet been inspected. No model filename, installation command, or successful run is asserted here.

Once the artifact is available, record the archive version and model license, use the NetLogo version required by that artifact, and follow its own setup and experiment instructions. Then select a named experiment and record the initial ecological state, fishing agents, policy setting, seeds, and output measures. Policy conclusions require a separate assessment of calibration and empirical support.
