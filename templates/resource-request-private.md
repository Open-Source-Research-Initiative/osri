Complete only what the request needs. The assigned administrator helps resolve unknowns. See the [workflow](../governance/resource-requests.md).

**Request reference:** Public discussion or issue only if safe, or a private reference.

**Requester, project lead, and resource administrator:** Names and agreed contact route.

**Resource and version:** Citation/provider, stable identifier, dataset or article version, and the exact material or query needed.

**Research purpose and intended users:** Who will use it, for which project, and what outputs are needed.

**Requested method:** Permitted copy/extract, approved direct access, or administrator-run query/analysis. Note planned software, agents, services, storage, and any transfer or export.

**Timing and cost:** Deadline, estimated cost if known, and who may authorize it.

**Open alternatives checked:** Suitability and material differences.
