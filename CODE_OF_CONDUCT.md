# Community conduct

Welcome people across fields and skill levels. Explain terms, ask questions, and criticize ideas and evidence respectfully. Give credit for contributions.

Do not harass people, share private information without permission, or misrepresent evidence and review. Keep discussion relevant to the research and collaboration.

Contact [Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu) about confidential concerns. The administrator may remove harmful content or limit participation. You may ask for an explanation or reconsideration. An independent appeal reviewer has not been appointed.
