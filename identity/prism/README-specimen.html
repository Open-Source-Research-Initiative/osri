Open Source Research Initiative

OSRI is a founder-led initiative seeking collaborators across fields. People define questions, direct Agentic-Powered-Research, and evaluate evidence and conclusions. The research record is tracked in Git.

Start with a question

Read the short research guide. Use the project template when ready. You can bring your own agent or work manually; no paid tool bundle is required.

Contribute

  • Bring a question: propose a research topic in Ideas.
  • Build on evidence: explore topics and resources.
  • Improve the shared methods: read the contribution standards in the OSRI hub.

How the work moves

Question → evidence → analysis → human review → writing → publication, with revision throughout.

People authorize publication. Keep sources, code, decisions, and versions traceable in Git.
