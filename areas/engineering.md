# Engineering

Systems Engineering, Engineering Management, Operations Research, and Decision Science.

Initial work excludes physical laboratory experiments.

Possible questions concern system architecture, reliability, risk, scheduling, queues, resource allocation, and decisions under uncertainty.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples E01 and E02 are in the [candidate catalog](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/topics/). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A study can span several areas and keep one research repository. Find studies in the [research directory](../projects/README.md); each repository lists its tasks in **Issues**. See the [program scope](README.md).
