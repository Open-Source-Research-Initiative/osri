# Engineering

Systems Engineering, Engineering Management, Operations Research, and Decision Science.

Possible questions concern system architecture, reliability, risk, scheduling, queues, resource allocation, and decisions under uncertainty.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples E01 and E02 are in the [candidate catalog](../topics/example-topics.csv). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A project can span several areas. It uses one canonical repository. See the [program scope](README.md) and [project directory](../projects/README.md).
