# Public Policy

Policy analysis, evaluation, governance, institutions, and public programs.

Possible questions concern technology policy, public information, implementation, resource use, and evaluation methods.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples P01 and P02 are in the [candidate catalog](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/topics/). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A study can span several areas and keep one research repository. Find studies in the [research directory](../projects/README.md); each repository lists its tasks in **Issues**. See the [program scope](README.md).
