# Social and Behavioral Sciences

Human judgment, collaboration, organizations, and behavior.

Possible questions concern survey interpretation, social models, coordination, and how people direct research with agents.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples S01 and S02 are in the [candidate catalog](../topics/example-topics.csv). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A project can span several areas. It uses one canonical repository. See the [program scope](README.md) and [project directory](../projects/README.md).
