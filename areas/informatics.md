# Informatics

Bioinformatics, health and research informatics, information systems, and Computational Natural Sciences.

Initial work excludes physical laboratory experiments.

Possible questions concern computational biology, chemistry, physics, materials, data methods, interoperability, and knowledge representation.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples I01 and I02 are in the [candidate catalog](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/topics/). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A project can span several areas. It uses one main project repository. See the [program scope](README.md) and [project directory](../projects/README.md).
