# Healthcare

Nonclinical Public Health and Health Systems Research.

Possible questions concern population health, service access, health policy, service delivery, and use of resources.

Start with [the research guide](../START_HERE.md), [resources](../resources/README.md), or [topic ideas](../topics/README.md). Examples H01 and H02 are in the [candidate catalog](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/topics/). They are ideas, not confirmed gaps.

A project can span several areas. It uses one main project repository. See the [program scope](README.md) and [project directory](../projects/README.md).
