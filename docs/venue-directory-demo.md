# Find and compare publication venues in 90 seconds

Start with a research field, compare up to three venues, and check the conditions before deciding where to submit. This is a directory walkthrough using a public-health example, not a recommendation for a particular manuscript or a report of researcher adoption.

The video is silent, with on-screen captions. The same steps are written below. [Watch or download the video](../site/assets/demos/venue-directory-demo.webm).

## 1. Choose a field

Open the [directory](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/). Start with [public health](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/?subfield=public-health) or [education and learning sciences](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/?subfield=education-learning-sciences).

The default requires verified free submission and a free publication route, compatible substantive AI use, peer review and documented core editorial standards. Each venue still has scope and eligibility conditions. Expanded browsing modes show incomplete checks and other records.

## 2. Compare up to three venues

Read the scope summaries, select **Compare** on up to three results, then choose **View comparison**. Selection is a way to inspect differences; it does not establish that all three fit your paper.

## 3. Check author costs separately

Open **Scope and costs** on a result or **Full cost conditions and sources** in the comparison. Read submission and publication costs separately, along with article types and any mandatory conditions. Optional open access can cost money while a standard author route is free. A reader subscription is a separate cost.

## 4. Read AI disclosure and restrictions

Open **AI use and disclosure**, then follow the official instructions. The directory checks substantive human-led research, analysis/code and drafting; the venue's restrictions and disclosure requirements still apply. A listed policy does not give unrestricted permission to generate a paper.

## 5. Save, share, or help

Copy the address-bar URL to keep the filters and selected venues. Reopening it restores the shortlist. There is no account or manuscript upload to save a selection.

[Report a source or correction](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/new?template=venue-evidence.yml), [share your experience](https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/new?template=directory-use.yml), or [choose a small contributor task](../community/venue-starter-tasks.md). A no-fit result helps us understand coverage gaps.

The recording uses the November 16, 2026 directory. Sources and venue instructions can change; read the current official guidance before submitting. Codex prepared the walkthrough and performed browser checks; these are separate from external human usability testing and scientific review.
