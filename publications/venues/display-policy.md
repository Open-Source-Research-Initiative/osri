# Directory browsing policy — November 16, 2026

The default shows verified free submission and publication, peer review, a compatible substantive AI workflow, and core editorial standards documented. There are currently 57 such venues. This is a starting set for manuscript-specific assessment, not a prediction of acceptance or endorsement of every article.

“Include incomplete quality checks” adds the 16 free venues with unresolved core checks, for 73 results before other filters. It excludes documented medium/high concerns. “All directory records” makes all 146 AI-compatible records inspectable, including conditional routes, unverified costs, and documented concerns. The 151 excluded candidates remain in the audit records rather than the browser.

The underlying 74 verified free author routes and all venue evidence are unchanged. This display policy supersedes the initial quality campaign's default, which included incomplete quality checks. Historical campaign records retain their original meaning. Counts are generated from the current records.

Comparison supports up to three venues. Filters and selected canonical venue IDs are stored in the URL, without an account or manuscript upload. The old summary key `verified_free_peer_reviewed_open_for_consideration` now follows the stricter default; `verified_free_core_documented` is its explicit equivalent. `verified_free_peer_reviewed_before_quality` continues to count author-cost verification before the editorial-quality filter.
