## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### Bulletin of the World Health Organization

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The official policy screens for originality, scientific rigor and international public-health relevance before external review. It requires explicit new-knowledge statements and rigorous, current systematic reviews. International relevance, lower-income-setting emphasis and study-country affiliation are fit/eligibility conditions. WHO identity and citation impact alone do not establish a comparative difficulty band; no acceptance cohort was verified.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. WHO charges no author fees. Research, systematic reviews and policy/practice articles normally have 3000-word limits; there is no paid excess-length route stated. Abstract translation is free. Supplementary material for accepted work must be placed in a DOI-issuing repository; use a free suitable repository. Authors obtain permissions for copyrighted reuse. Apply current affiliation, review-search recency and preprint restrictions.
Fully open access with no author fees. Research, systematic reviews and Policy & practice normally allow 3,000 words; follow format-specific limits. English submissions; translated abstracts cost authors nothing. Guidelines restrict prior publication, with a preprint exception only for research in a public health emergency of international concern. Primary research requires study-country institutional representation.

**Fit:** Fits health-policy, operational, implementation and systems research with international public-health significance, especially for vulnerable/lower-income populations. Research, systematic reviews and Policy & practice are peer reviewed. Review protocols should be registered; searches must be multilingual and within six months. Research needs study-country institutional representation; the general authorship policy also calls for study-country professional affiliation. Disclose any AI use and its purpose. Prior preprints are excepted only for research in a public-health emergency of international concern. Clinical interventions remain outside OSRI scope.

### Health Information Management Journal

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Official instructions require original work that advances health-information-management knowledge or practice, critical literature reviews and appropriate methods. They also accommodate smaller professional-practice and innovation reports. These article-specific criteria and the review procedure do not establish relative selectivity or acceptance probability; no comparative band is justified by the evidence inspected.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Standard publication has no submission or publication fee. OA is optional and can be charged; print color specifically requested by the author can also be charged. Research and literature-review articles allow 5,000 words excluding abstract and references. Online color is available under the standard route.

**Fit:** Fits health-information management, coding, data quality, governance and policy research using existing records or evidence synthesis. International submissions are welcomed; work should enhance health-information management knowledge or practice. Conference-derived work must be significantly extended and supplied for overlap assessment. Substantive GenAI use requires disclosure and output verification; the publisher prohibits specified content/data uses. Independent authors are eligible, subject to possible additional integrity documentation.

### Pan American Journal of Public Health / Revista Panamericana de Salud Pública

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Official selection criteria include scientific soundness, originality, salience, timeliness and applicability beyond the study setting across the Americas. An editorial merit screen precedes at least three external reviewers. Regional relevance is a fit requirement, and review count is not selectivity. No current acceptance cohort or adequate comparative evidence was verified to establish a difficulty band.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Diamond OA policy excludes fees for researchers and readers and says publication costs authors nothing. No selective waiver or membership is involved. Original research and reviews allow 3,500 words excluding abstract/tables/figures/references; shorter types have lower limits. Accepts English, Portuguese and Spanish; disclose any prior preprint or repository publication.

**Fit:** Fits policy, workforce, methods, systems and service research relevant to the Americas, including critical reviews and analytical reports. Clinical case reports and noncritical descriptive reviews are excluded. AI use beyond light editing needs Methods details and a final AI-use declaration, independent human output verification and author responsibility; LLMs cannot be authors. Disclose prior preprints/repository versions with a link or copy; this disclosure rule is not blanket approval of prior publication.

### Q Bulletin

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Completed QA/QI projects from the past five years with at least two improvement cycles are an eligibility requirement, not a prestige score. The official policy describes editorial screening and two independent reviewers, but provides no observed acceptance cohort or contribution-selectivity threshold that supports relative difficulty. No assumption of easy acceptance follows from its regional or institutional identity.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Ministry-funded diamond route expressly excludes submission, processing and publication fees. Only qualifying completed QA/QI projects are eligible: completion within five years and at least two improvement cycles. Main text is approximately 5,000 words excluding abstract/tables/figures; at most five figures/tables; appendices at most five pages.

**Fit:** Narrow fit for reports of existing completed healthcare service-process QA/QI projects using the journal's accepted methodology. Projects must have finished within five years and include at least two improvement cycles. Generic simulations, unimplemented proposals, evidence syntheses, and observational analyses without the required project cycles are not established fits. OSRI's route would concern existing nonclinical service-improvement work.
