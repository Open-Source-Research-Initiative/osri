## Health Information Management Journal

**Fit:** Fits health-information management, coding, data quality, governance and policy research using existing records or evidence synthesis. International submissions are welcomed; work should enhance health-information management knowledge or practice. Conference-derived work must be significantly extended and supplied for overlap assessment. Substantive GenAI use requires disclosure and output verification; the publisher prohibits specified content/data uses. Independent authors are eligible, subject to possible additional integrity documentation.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Standard publication has no submission or publication fee. OA is optional and can be charged; print color specifically requested by the author can also be charged. Research and literature-review articles allow 5,000 words excluding abstract and references. Online color is available under the standard route. No compulsory membership, registration, page or overlength payment is stated for manuscripts within the article limits; journal policy expressly excludes submission and publication fees.

**AI policy:** Human-led literature assistance and generated or checked research code are expressly listed as appropriate subject to disclosure. Conditional partial substantive drafting is inferred from the treatment of primary or partial generated text, the statement that disclosed use alone is not grounds for rejection, and the non-exhaustive examples; the policy does not expressly list drafting as an appropriate example. Authors remain responsible for accuracy and original sources.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose generated content that directly affects methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission for editorial evaluation; appropriate research uses are disclosed in methods or acknowledgements. Suggestions to improve the author’s language, grammar or structure need no disclosure. Verify outputs and original sources.

**Fit:** Fits policy, workforce, methods, systems and service research relevant to the Americas, including critical reviews and analytical reports. Clinical case reports and noncritical descriptive reviews are excluded. AI use beyond light editing needs Methods details and a final AI-use declaration, independent human output verification and author responsibility; LLMs cannot be authors. Disclose prior preprints/repository versions with a link or copy; this disclosure rule is not blanket approval of prior publication.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Diamond open access without author/researcher charges or selective fee waiver. English, Portuguese and Spanish manuscripts are accepted. Follow article-specific length and table/reference limits in the full author instructions; no paid excess-page option is stated. Authors obtain permissions for copyrighted reuse and meet data-availability requirements. Regional public-health relevance remains essential.
