## Progress in Energy

**Fit:** Research and synthesis across the global energy transition; computational or existing-data studies must meet its significance requirements.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Standard subscription publication is free; optional gold open access is paid. Research papers require a justification statement; all reviews and perspectives require an outline before writing. Journal explicitly says no other publishing charges.

**AI policy:** IOP permits AI-assisted research ideation, methods, analysis, code generation and text generation when appropriate, disclosed and validated. Authors must critically revise generated text and retain scientific reasoning, interpretation and accountability.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** For material research or manuscript contributions include an AI disclosure in Acknowledgements identifying tool or model, use and extent of verification, validation and critical review. Research use also requires details in Methods. Routine nonmaterial spelling, grammar and language editing is exempt; prompts and settings may be required where central to methods.
