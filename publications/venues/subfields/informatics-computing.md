# Computer science, AI, data and research software

Area: Informatics. Checked November 16, 2026. These are venue comparisons, not an acceptance prediction. [Methodology](../methodology.md).

## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Agent judgment within AI methods: the current transparency page reports strong overall modeled selection and screening for results important to the AI community, including methodological advances beyond a domain application. Its overall and post-screening probabilities refer to different populations; neither predicts the fate of an individual paper.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. No submission or publication fees. The ordinary journal route needs no conference participation. Authors supply publication-ready files and the mandatory reproducibility checklist; surveys have separate contribution requirements. Long articles may take longer to review, without a paid length option in the inspected policy. AI may assist research, but core contributions must come from the human authors.

**Fit:** AI methods, reasoning, knowledge representation, agents, NLP, planning, vision and learning. Research articles, analytical surveys and technical notes must advance understanding beyond a narrow application.

### TheoretiCS

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Agent judgment within theoretical computer science: Phase 1 explicitly asks whether work is among the strongest contributions in its area in roughly the last year, emphasizing significance and lasting value before full correctness review. This concrete threshold supports very_high. The incomplete 2025 cohort is not used to estimate acceptance odds.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Free diamond Episciences journal. First post the article and LaTeX source on arXiv/CoRR, then submit the correct version through an Episciences account. No page limit applies. Only original research articles are accepted. Authors must be natural persons and disclose contributions by category of AI use. Conference versions are permitted; conference registration is not required for this journal route.

**Fit:** Original research across theoretical computer science, including full versions of conference papers. Articles need complete proofs and a contribution of lasting significance. Review articles are not a stated article type.

### Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Agent judgment within graph-algorithms research: the journal explicitly selects a limited set of papers and says new/correct results alone are insufficient; work needs sufficient weight, interest and potential to stimulate further research. Preliminary screening can reject marginal-interest or insufficiently original papers. This supports a significance-selective band without numerical odds.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Diamond journal with no author charges. Requires LaTeX, numbered pages and lines, and journal-class final sources. Concise papers normally span 5–10 pages; the inspected regular-paper instructions do not specify a paid overlength option. Declare all generative AI use in submission comments; humans must validate its output and unattributed AI creation of scientific content is forbidden. Ordinary submission requires no conference attendance.

**Fit:** Design, analysis, implementation and applications of graph algorithms; includes network analysis, graph drawing, database and knowledge-representation algorithms, software engineering and biomedical-informatics applications when graph-algorithm research is central. Regular, concise and survey papers are eligible.

### Transactions on Machine Learning Research

**Difficulty:** moderate (provisional agent judgment). Agent judgment within ML research, based on an explicitly less significance-selective acceptance model: accurate convincing evidence, clear communication and findings of interest to some ML researchers. Method novelty, state-of-the-art results, significance and impact are not necessary acceptance gates. Technical rigor remains necessary; this band gives no numerical odds.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. No author fees. Every author needs an active OpenReview profile and sufficient annual submission quota, including for desk-rejected submissions. Length must be justified. No surveys from September 1, 2026; no expanded archival conference papers. The editorial LLM policy allows assistive use with human responsibility; the FAQ additionally expects human-sourced ideas/claims/results and first-page disclosure. Assess both against the actual workflow. Journal-to-conference presentation and attendance are separate.

**Fit:** Original ML studies, methods, reproducibility and applications yielding insight into learning methods. Survey submissions are excluded from September 1, 2026, despite a Survey Certification still appearing in the editorial page. Archival conference-paper extensions are excluded; preprints and expressly non-archival workshop overlap can be eligible. Current official pages differ in specificity on LLM use: the editorial policy permits assistive tools, while the FAQ expects human-sourced ideas, claims and results and requires first-page disclosure.

- [Fee evidence](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html) (2026-11-16, page): Any manuscript length is permitted if justified; active author profiles and the required format are documented.
- [Fee evidence](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/faq.html) (2026-11-16, page): Excludes surveys from September 1, 2026; distinguishes conference presentation opportunities and states human-contribution and LLM-disclosure expectations.

Several named sections are closed and ACIS selected papers require invitation.

### Journal of Open Source Software

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Agent judgment withheld for relative editorial selectivity.
