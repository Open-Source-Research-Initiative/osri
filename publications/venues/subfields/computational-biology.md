### Biodiversity Informatics

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Regular articles must materially advance biodiversity informatics; merely applying an existing model or repeating known phenomena may fall short. This establishes a contribution gate, but the different article formats and absent comparative acceptance data do not justify the previous high band.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The author-information page excludes all publishing-related fees; current instructions also exclude page charges. No waiver, paid membership or author-affiliation restriction is stated. Journal registration is required. Follow manuscript/software/data requirements and noncommercial Creative Commons terms.

**Fit:** Creation, integration, analysis and visualization of biodiversity information, including scholarly dataset work, assessments of information completeness, and software or protocol descriptions. Existing biodiversity datasets and substantive modelling/informatics advances fit; this is not a general molecular-bioinformatics journal. Research receives detailed peer review, normally with two anonymous reviewers.

### BIOMATH

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Nonroutine mathematical contribution or novel biological application is required. The officially reported 2025 acceptance figure and historical aggregate do not specify their denominator, submission-versus-decision cohort or handling of withdrawals. Preserve those qualified source statements as context; they do not establish a calibrated high band or manuscript-specific probability.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The standard online route is free for authors. No waiver, institutional affiliation or paid membership condition is stated. Printed copies are optional; author-copy postage is payable if requested. Follow the manuscript format.

**Fit:** Mathematical results and nonroutine mathematical methods for biosciences, or novel biological applications that identify a need for new mathematical approaches. Theory, simulation, biological networks and existing-data modelling can fit; a routine model application without substantive mathematical or biological contribution may not. Research articles undergo blind review by at least two independent reviewers.

### Biometrical Letters

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The current official guide requires rigorous statistical/mathematical work that advances or meaningfully applies methodology to life-science problems, with editor screening before double-blind review. The publishing platform also gives a 20% rejection figure with no period or cohort. Those incomplete statistics and broad quality/fit criteria do not support a reliable comparative difficulty band; no complementary acceptance percentage is inferred.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The guide gives a blanket no-charge policy for authors/readers, covering the standard submission/publication route without a waiver. No mandatory membership is stated. Prepare identified and anonymous versions; required template/copyright links currently contain placeholders. CC BY-NC-ND licensing may affect funder fit.

**Fit:** Statistical and mathematical methodology for life sciences, new statistical tools motivated by biological problems, and substantive applications or reviews of data-analysis methods. Existing biological datasets and simulation studies can fit. The journal encourages reproducible code and data and describes double-blind peer review. AI may assist under human supervision and requires a manuscript-preparation declaration, with an exemption for basic grammar/spelling/reference tools. The current guide prohibits generative AI use by reviewers or editors during manuscript evaluation; authors remain responsible.

### Journal of Open Source Software

**Difficulty:** not comparable (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent assessment for the software artifact route: JOSS publishes peer-reviewed, reusable research software rather than a biological findings paper. Current policy requires demonstrated research use/impact, meaningful intellectual/design contribution, feature completeness, documentation and tests; its pre-review gate requires more than six months of active public development. Minor utilities, isolated notebooks and unproven future-use claims do not qualify. Its software-maturity burden is substantial and cannot be represented as easy acceptance or placed on a biological-discovery prestige ladder.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. No fees to submit or publish. Requires mature, research-used software under an OSI-approved license, tests, documentation and more than six months of active public development. A major contributor submits, participates in public review and archives a tagged release with a DOI. AI assistance requires disclosure and human validation; AI-mediated author/editor or reviewer conversation is prohibited except translation. Models, notebooks, thin API clients and one-off analysis tools are outside scope.
No submission or publication fees. Software must meet research-impact, feature-completeness and open-development gates, including more than six months of active public development under current pre-review criteria. Major-contributor authors participate through GitHub; archive the reviewed release with a DOI using a free archive. Disclose AI tools, use and human validation. AI use in conversational interactions between authors and editors/reviewers is prohibited except translation. Human authors must make core design decisions and take responsibility.

**Fit:** A cross-disciplinary research-software journal explicitly covering biology and scientific modelling/data analysis. A substantive, reusable computational-biology package can fit. Papers describe software and its research purpose rather than presenting new biological research results. Standalone notebooks, pretrained models and minor utility packages are outside the stated scope. Both software and the accompanying paper receive open external peer review. Current pre-review gates require more than six months of active public history and demonstrated research use; prospective usefulness alone is insufficient. AI-assisted code/paper work needs disclosure and human validation, and AI use in conversational interactions with editors/reviewers is prohibited except translation.

### Peer Community Journal

**Difficulty:** not comparable (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent assessment: journal publication is available after a thematic PCI recommendation, so acceptance at the final transfer stage cannot represent the difficulty of obtaining that recommendation. PCJ requires high-quality, methodologically/ethically sound research; evaluation is not guaranteed because a competent recommender must first find the preprint interesting. At least two reliable review reports and an editorial decision are required. Compare the relevant computational-biology PCI process and manuscript contribution, not an unconditional-post-recommendation transfer rate.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The entire PCI evaluation and journal-publication process is free for all authors. First deposit an accessible preprint and obtain a recommendation from an appropriate PCI, then opt for journal publication. Prior recommendation and open data/code are scholarly prerequisites, not financial waivers. Choose free required repositories.

**Fit:** A multidisciplinary journal with a Mathematical and Computational Biology community and relevant genomics, ecology and evolutionary-biology communities. Theory, computer or mathematical models, existing-data studies and methodological work can fit the selected community. Articles must first complete the thematic Peer Community In review and recommendation process; the journal does not take an unevaluated manuscript directly. The final journal step publishes the recommended version without another peer-review round; review provenance comes from the thematic PCI, not from preprint deposit alone.

[Author guidance](https://peercommunityjournal.org/page/about/) · [Venue](https://peercommunityjournal.org/)

- [Fee evidence](https://peercommunityin.org/) (2026-11-16, page): Publisher explicitly makes the complete PCI/Peer Community Journal process free for every user, including authors.
- [Fee evidence](https://peercommunityjournal.org/page/about/) (2026-11-16, page): Browser returned 403, but direct HTTPS readback succeeded (HTTP 200): no author fees; prior PCI recommendation; no article-length/figure/table restriction.
- Difficulty evidence: [source 1](https://peercommunityjournal.org/page/about/), [source 2](https://peercommunityin.org/pc-journal/)
