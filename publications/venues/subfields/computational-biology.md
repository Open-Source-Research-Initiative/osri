## Journal of Open Source Software

**Fit:** A cross-disciplinary research-software journal explicitly covering biology and scientific modelling/data analysis. A substantive, reusable computational-biology package can fit. Papers describe software and its research purpose rather than presenting new biological research results. Standalone notebooks, pretrained models and minor utility packages are outside the stated scope. Both software and the accompanying paper receive open external peer review. Current pre-review gates require more than six months of active public history and demonstrated research use; prospective usefulness alone is insufficient. AI-assisted code/paper work needs disclosure and human validation, and AI use in conversational interactions with editors/reviewers is prohibited except translation.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** No fees to submit or publish. Requires mature, research-used software under an OSI-approved license, tests, documentation and more than six months of active public development. A major contributor submits, participates in public review and archives a tagged release with a DOI. AI assistance requires disclosure and human validation; AI-mediated author/editor or reviewer conversation is prohibited except translation. Models, notebooks, thin API clients and one-off analysis tools are outside scope.
No submission or publication fees. Software must meet research-impact, feature-completeness and open-development gates, including more than six months of active public development under current pre-review criteria. Major-contributor authors participate through GitHub; archive the reviewed release with a DOI using a free archive. Disclose AI tools, use and human validation. AI use in conversational interactions between authors and editors/reviewers is prohibited except translation. Human authors must make core design decisions and take responsibility.
Journal states no fees for manuscript processing or publishing and zero publication fees. Research software must satisfy submission requirements. None in the journal submission/publication policy. Repository hosting must permit free public access; free supported archive choices exist. Existing software development expenses are not journal charges.

**AI policy:** Most aspects of submission including software creation, review, and paper authoring may use generative AI; humans review and validate outputs and make core design decisions.

**Disclosure (required):** Include an AI usage disclosure statement identifying tools/models and versions, where used, nature and scope of assistance, and confirmation that humans reviewed, edited and validated outputs and made core design decisions.

## Peer Community Journal

**Fit:** A multidisciplinary journal with a Mathematical and Computational Biology community and relevant genomics, ecology and evolutionary-biology communities. Theory, computer or mathematical models, existing-data studies and methodological work can fit the selected community. Articles must first complete the thematic Peer Community In review and recommendation process; the journal does not take an unevaluated manuscript directly. The final journal step publishes the recommended version without another peer-review round; review provenance comes from the thematic PCI, not from preprint deposit alone.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** The entire PCI evaluation and journal-publication process is free for all authors. First deposit an accessible preprint and obtain a recommendation from an appropriate PCI, then opt for journal publication. Prior recommendation and open data/code are scholarly prerequisites, not financial waivers. Choose free required repositories. None charged by PCI or the journal; no mandatory membership. Journal policy imposes no article-length or figure/table-count restriction. Avoid optional paid external services.

**AI policy:** Permits AI bibliography and data analysis with published/cited or adequately described methods, and generation of text and code with checking.

**Disclosure (required):** Disclose how AI generated text and code in Methods or Acknowledgements; document and cite analytical methods sufficiently.
