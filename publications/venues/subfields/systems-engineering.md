## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### International Journal of Industrial Engineering and Management

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Official scope welcomes original theoretical/application-oriented work, surveys and cases, but neither a defined acceptance rate nor a distinguishing selectivity threshold was located. Citation quartiles do not determine acceptance difficulty.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Official instructions exclude submission, review, and publication charges. English manuscripts should be 4500–6000 words and at most 15 pages including illustrations/appendices. No compulsory membership, waiver, affiliation, or conference attendance condition is stated for this journal route.

**Fit:** Industrial engineering and production management, including engineering/project management, maintenance, quality, logistics, service engineering, and industrial AI. The scope explicitly includes theoretical/application research, surveys, and critical reviews; computational analyses and synthesis need a substantive industrial or production-management contribution.

### Journal of Engineering Management and Competitiveness

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The official policy describes scope screening and at least two independent reviewers assessing originality, contribution and scholarly relevance; it does not establish comparative selectivity or an acceptance rate. A difficulty band would overstate this evidence.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Official policy excludes submission, processing, publication, and hidden costs. No maximum length is set; length relative to substance is reviewed. Research AI requires detailed disclosure; manuscript AI is limited and generated intellectual content prohibited. Copyright retention in policy conflicts with transfer language in instructions.

**Fit:** Engineering management, maintenance/risk/quality management, simulation, operations research, statistical models, and management information systems. Computational methods, existing-data studies, and synthesis can fit the engineering management contribution. Original, review, and short scientific papers are considered. AI-assisted research is allowed only with scientific justification and detailed disclosure of tool, provider, access date, prompts and purpose; manuscript intellectual content must be the authors' own. The source distinguishes research use from restricted manuscript-generation use.

[Author guidance](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/InstructionsForAuthors.html) · [Venue](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/)

- [Fee evidence](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/JournalPolicy.html) (2026-11-16, page): Article-processing-charge section expressly makes manuscript submission, processing, and article publication free and rules out hidden costs.
- [Fee evidence](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/InstructionsForAuthors.html) (2026-11-16, page): Instructions set no maximum length but require concision and third-party reuse permissions; their copyright-transfer wording conflicts with the policy.
- [Submission to publication (policy-stated average days)](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/JournalPolicy.html): 90; year/period: not reported; population: not reported. Undated policy average; measurement period, sample and treatment of rejected/withdrawn papers are unstated.
- Difficulty evidence: [source 1](https://www.tfzr.rs/jemc/JournalPolicy.html)

### Journal of System Safety

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Original/review articles must advance or critically review system-safety scholarship and are peer reviewed, but no official acceptance statistics or sufficiently differentiating selectivity evidence were located. Peer review alone does not establish a band.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Official guidelines exclude APCs, submission fees, and every other author payment. Account registration and a free ORCID are required; no membership or conference attendance condition is stated. Original and review articles are peer reviewed. Authors must obtain rights for reused content.

**Fit:** System safety theory and methods, quantitative risk assessment, resilience, software safety, AI safety, and STPA. OSRI-fit examples include computational hazard analysis, existing-data studies, and literature synthesis. This record covers original and review articles; columns, letters, and editorials are not peer reviewed.

[Author guidance](https://jsystemsafety.com/index.php/jss/author-guidelines) · [Venue](https://jsystemsafety.com/index.php/jss)

- [Fee evidence](https://jsystemsafety.com/index.php/jss/author-guidelines) (2026-11-16, page): Journal-specific costs section expressly excludes submission fees, APCs, and any other author payments; original/review article types are distinguished from unreviewed content.
- Difficulty evidence: [source 1](https://jsystemsafety.com/index.php/jss/author-guidelines)

### Serbian Journal of Management

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Official pages establish original management research and double-blind review, but no published acceptance statistic or sufficiently distinguishing contribution threshold was verified. Citation metrics do not establish editorial difficulty.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Official instructions explicitly exclude APCs and submission charges and state publication is free. Research articles should be under 15 pages including tables/figures, reviews at most 20 pages, and special-issue papers at most 10 pages. No waiver, compulsory membership, or conference registration is stated.

**Fit:** Management science theory and practice. Engineering management methods, existing-data organizational research, decision methods, and systematic reviews are plausible fits when their management contribution is clear; this is broader management coverage rather than a specialist systems architecture journal.

[Author guidance](https://www.sjm06.com/instr_auth.html) · [Venue](https://www.sjm06.com/)

- [Fee evidence](https://www.sjm06.com/instr_auth.html) (2026-11-16, page): Instructions explicitly exclude article processing and submission charges and state that manuscript publication is free; give research/review length limits and double-blind review.
- [Journal Impact Factor (publisher-labeled ESCI IF)](https://www.sjm06.com/): 1.1; year/period: 2025; population: not reported. Official homepage reports ESCI IF (2025); JCR was not independently queried.
- [SJR (publisher-labeled)](https://www.sjm06.com/): 0.27; year/period: 2025; population: not reported. Homepage labels this SCOPUS Scimago IF and SJR category Q3; country-list link is nonspecific and blocked. Preserve labeling caveat.
