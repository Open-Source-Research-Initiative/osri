## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### SORT (Statistics and Operations Research Transactions)

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within statistics and operations research: the official journal statistics report rejection of 83.3% in 2025 and 91.0% in 2024 relative to total articles submitted. Alongside the original-article and specialist-review requirements, this is direct evidence of substantial editorial selectivity. Submission counts, pending cases and cohort rules are not given; rejection percentages are not converted to acceptance percentages or individual odds.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. English-language original articles; an online RACO account is required without a stated payment. Normally at most about 25 PDF pages, or 40 pages using the SORT LaTeX template, including figures, tables and references; justified longer articles are possible. LaTeX is recommended initially and required at revision. The general no-fee statement is not a selective waiver.

**Fit:** Original methodological or applied statistics and operations research, including computational or educational elements. Real application examples are encouraged. Suitable for decision-science methods, stochastic modeling and existing-data studies with a substantive statistical or OR contribution.

### Stochastic Systems

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within applied probability and stochastic operations research: regular papers must make a substantial theoretical, methodological or practical contribution. The associate editor may reject even a mathematically correct paper for insufficient contribution; this is stronger evidence than routine peer review alone. No official acceptance rate or citation metric was located in the current journal homepage and publisher metrics index inspected.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The current journal instructions explicitly exclude APCs and submission fees. Regular manuscripts are limited to 35 pages including references; short correspondences to eight pages. No author membership, registration payment or institutional agreement is required by the stated route.

**Fit:** Applied probability, queues, stochastic models, computational methods and applications in service operations, logistics, computer systems and related OR domains. Regular manuscripts require a substantive contribution; short correspondences are also accepted. Computational health-system work fits without clinical practice.

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The current OJS policy requires an original OR contribution, initial screening and two external experts using double-blind review. The society homepage reports 48% acceptance and 52% rejection for 2020, but does not define their populations; those historical figures are not current selectivity evidence. No present-day comparative difficulty band is justified.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Ordinary journal submission, without conference attendance. Manuscripts should contain 10–12 pages including bibliography, with at most 30 references. **Fit:** Original OR theory and applications, optimization, stochastic models, simulation, quantitative decision support and related methods. Computational studies and existing-data applications fit; the instructions require an original scientific contribution.
