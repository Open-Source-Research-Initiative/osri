# Meta-research, evidence synthesis and scholarly communication

Area: Cross-cutting. Checked November 16, 2026. These are venue comparisons, not an acceptance prediction. [Methodology](../methodology.md).

## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### Journal of Information Science

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within information-science research: contributions must advance thinking in the field and pass scope/quality screening; research manuscripts require at least two referees and most technical papers seek five reviews. The official 4.6% acceptance rate across all final decisions supports very high selectivity within this community. Bibliometric subject matter alone does not establish fit. No manuscript-specific acceptance odds or cross-discipline prestige ordering is claimed.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Current instructions explicitly exclude submission and publication fees for the ordinary subscription route. OA through Sage Choice is optional and separately charged or agreement-funded. Online colour is free; requested print colour may be charged. Ordinary papers average 5,000–7,500 words, with brief communications up to 1,500. No compulsory membership or affiliation is imposed in the inspected author route.

**Fit:** Information science and knowledge management offer a route for computational analyses of scholarly information, research-information organization, retrieval and communication when there is a clear information-science contribution. Methods and algorithms can accompany a research article; this is not a standalone software-deposit venue. Average article length is 5,000–7,500 words, with brief communications up to 1,500. Preprints are accepted, with no updates during review and a link to the published version after acceptance. The linked Sage AI policy distinguishes assistive editing from generative research/content uses requiring disclosure and prohibits specified uses; human authors remain accountable.

### Social Studies of Science

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within generalist Science and Technology Studies: the journal seeks original work advancing understanding of science, technology and medicine as social/material activities and screens for scope and quality before review. Its officially displayed 7.7% acceptance rate, with all final decisions as denominator, supports very high selectivity for that editorial community. It is not a prediction for a particular manuscript or a comparison with citation impact in other disciplines.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Journal-specific instructions explicitly exclude fees for submission and publication on the subscription route. Optional Sage Choice OA is paid or separately agreement-funded. Online colour is free; specifically requested print colour can be charged. Research articles are normally limited to 12,000 words; follow editorial length requirements. No mandatory membership or affiliation is imposed in the inspected author route.

**Fit:** Science and Technology Studies rather than a general methods journal. Social or institutional analyses of research practices, collaboration and knowledge production may fit when they make an STS contribution. Original research, discussion papers, short research notes and review essays are listed; existing literature or documents can support suitable studies. Research articles are normally limited to 12,000 words. Preprints are accepted; do not update the preprint during review and link the published version afterward. The linked Sage AI policy distinguishes assistive editing from generative research/content uses requiring disclosure and prohibits specified uses; it does not grant blanket permission for AI-authored submissions.

### Research Ethics

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within research-ethics and integrity scholarship: scope and quality screening precede anonymized peer review, and reviews must critically analyze a substantive ethics/integrity issue. The official 21.9% acceptance rate supports a selective route alongside those contribution requirements. The rate covers all article types and final decisions, so it is not the probability of acceptance for an original research paper. Letters may receive editorial review.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Current Community Open Access policy explicitly excludes author costs and an APC after acceptance; support does not require the submitting author's institution to participate. The journal is online-only. Empirical original articles normally permit 7,000 words, theoretical articles 5,000 and reviews 3,000–6,000, excluding references; longer work needs editorial justification. All submissions require an AI-use statement, including when no AI was used.

**Fit:** Research ethics, scientific integrity, research regulation and ethics-review processes. Theoretical original articles and critical reviews allow work based on existing evidence; new clinical studies are unnecessary. Original empirical articles normally have a 7,000-word limit, theoretical articles 5,000, and reviews 3,000–6,000. Letters receive editorial review and are not automatically peer-reviewed research articles. An AI statement is mandatory in every submission, including an explicit statement of no AI use where applicable. Preprints are accepted but must not be updated during review; accepted work requires a final-publication link. The journal-specific AI statement requirement is stricter than Sage's general assistive-editing disclosure exemption.

### Engaging Science, Technology, and Society

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Skeptical agent judgment: broad STS relevance, adequate argument and readable presentation are substantive editorial gates, normally followed by at least two peer-review reports. They establish manuscript fit and scholarly expectations, but do not by themselves establish high comparative selectivity. No defined official acceptance statistic was verified. Restrictive genAI expectations are a separate fit condition, not selectivity evidence.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Current OA policy explicitly excludes submission fees and APCs without required membership or affiliation. The current single contribution category permits up to 9,000 words including abstract/footnotes but excluding references; overlong manuscripts are returned for revision. Most genAI use is strongly discouraged, with translation when alternatives are unavailable and AI-focused research identified as exceptions. All uses must be disclosed in the cover letter; data-generation or analysis uses must also be described in manuscript Methods. Human authors remain responsible.

**Fit:** Conceptually grounded Science and Technology Studies work must matter to a broad STS readership, including studies of research cultures, institutions, knowledge and science/society relations. This is not a general statistics or synthesis-methods venue. From 2026, current submission instructions consolidate contributions into a single category up to 9,000 words. External double-anonymous review is the general rule, with stated exceptions such as single-anonymous review when anonymization is impossible or time-sensitive editorial review. Preprints are encouraged with disclosure and final-publication linking. Most genAI use is strongly discouraged and every use needs disclosure.

### European Science Editing

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Original articles have priority and must contribute to scholarly editing/publishing practice; suitable original articles receive at least two independent expert reviews, while reviews and viewpoints require at least one. Official instructions give an approximate acceptance percentage without a year or denominator. That incomplete statistic and routine review requirements do not support a comparative difficulty band.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Current official submission and ARPHA instructions explicitly exclude submission/publication fees and author charges. No required EASE membership, affiliation or waiver is stated. Original articles and reviews have 4,000-word main-body limits and format-specific reference/illustration limits. The tailored publicity service is optional and paid. Follow current AI disclosure and human-accountability requirements, including disclosure of tool, task and relevant prompts.

**Fit:** Original contributions to scientific/scholarly editing and publishing, including integrity, peer review, bibliometrics, open science, predatory publishing and statistics, plus writing and translation. Existing-data studies and reviews require relevance to editing/publishing practice; this is not a general venue for every computational analysis. Original articles are prioritized; original articles and reviews have 4,000-word main-body limits. Suitable originals receive at least two independent reviewers; reviews/viewpoints at least one. Preprints are considered with disclosure and final-publication linking. AI uses require tool/task disclosure in the manuscript and prompt details in supplements; human authors remain accountable.

### Meta-Psychology

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Skeptical agent judgment: absence of a publication quota, target rejection rate or novelty criterion does not establish moderate acceptance difficulty. Papers still need a distinct psychological-science contribution, desk screening, at least two expert reviews and applicable reproducibility checks. The operative submission checklist requires preregistration for data-collecting or confirmatory studies. No sufficiently defined official acceptance statistic or comparative evidence was verified, so the band is unknown.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The official policy calls the journal completely free and places this under a no-processing-fees diamond-OA heading; this blanket statement is interpreted to cover submission as well as publication. No selective waiver, membership or paid editing service is required. Authors supply formatting/copyediting effort, a public DOI preprint and an OSF project; transparent review and a public cover letter apply. New-submission processing pauses annually in July. Current submission instructions require preregistration for data collection, explicitly including non-primary data collection in metascience/literature reviews, and for confirmatory questions/hypotheses. The About page still calls preregistration nonmandatory, so that conflict must be resolved for a specific manuscript.
Current journal policy describes a completely free diamond OA journal with no processing or publication fees; this blanket policy covers the author route without a selective waiver, membership or affiliation condition. Authors help format accepted papers; the journal has no print edition. Required OSF materials and a DOI-bearing preprint, plus preregistration where applicable, are author responsibilities rather than journal charges. New submissions pause during July.

**Fit:** Psychology-focused metascience: reviews/meta-analyses, replication and reproducibility reports, methods, tutorials and research software must contribute to psychological science. Current submission instructions require a public OSF project with applicable data/code/materials or an explanation of constraints, plus a DOI-bearing preprint. Studies collecting primary or secondary data, explicitly including metascience and literature reviews, or having confirmatory questions/hypotheses must be preregistered; ordinary commentaries/tutorials are exempt unless they do those things. Open named peer review includes at least two experts and applicable statistical reproducibility checks. Authors assist with formatting. The Questionable Research Ideas and Other Peculiarities section does not promise peer review and is outside this standard scholarly route.

### Science Editing

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Official acceptance criteria require research quality, originality and scientific significance, with at least two reviews and statistical review where needed. Original articles and other specified types may be unsolicited; reviews and several other types are commissioned or invited. These establish a real scholarly bar, but the displayed rolling acceptance statistic lacks a defined population and exact window dates, so a comparative difficulty band is left unknown.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Current author instructions explicitly exclude submission, page and article-processing charges; best-practice policy excludes author-side fees. No required membership or institutional agreement is stated. Original articles have a 2,500-word text limit; reviews normally allow 5,000 words and are described as invited. Follow format limits rather than assume a paid overlength option.

**Fit:** Scientific, technical and medical journal editing/publishing, including publishing data mining, integrity, peer review, journal evaluation and bibliometrics/scientometrics in the context of journal editing and publishing. Original analyses using existing records can fit; general subject-matter meta-analysis or bibliography is not sufficient without publishing/editing relevance. Unsolicited Original Articles allow 2,500 text words and 20 references under current guidance. Reviews normally allow 5,000 words and are invited. Unsolicited work receives external double-anonymous review, normally at least two reports and statistical review if needed; commissioned material may use editorial-board review.
