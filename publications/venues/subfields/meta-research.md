## Journal of Information Science

**Fit:** Information science and knowledge management offer a route for computational analyses of scholarly information, research-information organization, retrieval and communication when there is a clear information-science contribution. Methods and algorithms can accompany a research article; this is not a standalone software-deposit venue. Average article length is 5,000–7,500 words, with brief communications up to 1,500. Preprints are accepted, with no updates during review and a link to the published version after acceptance. The linked Sage AI policy distinguishes assistive editing from generative research/content uses requiring disclosure and prohibits specified uses; human authors remain accountable.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Current instructions explicitly exclude submission and publication fees for the ordinary subscription route. OA through Sage Choice is optional and separately charged or agreement-funded. Online colour is free; requested print colour may be charged. Ordinary papers average 5,000–7,500 words, with brief communications up to 1,500. No compulsory membership or affiliation is imposed in the inspected author route. None for the standard route; optional OA and requested print colour are separate.

**AI policy:** Human-led literature assistance and AI-generated or checked research code are appropriate with disclosure. Conditional partial drafting is inferred from Sage’s treatment of partial generated text and disclosed use, subject to editorial interpretation of its prohibition on generating a submission through prompts. Authors are responsible for accuracy and checking original sources.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose primary or partial generated content affecting methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission; report appropriate research uses in Methods or Acknowledgements. Suggestions to improve the author’s own language, grammar or structure do not require disclosure.
