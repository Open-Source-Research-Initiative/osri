## Contextus: Contemporary Journal of Economics and Management

**Fit:** Economics and management research including analysis, modeling, and technological articles.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Submit the manuscript in English for the free route: the journal charges no fees for submission, review, editing or publication. Accepted Portuguese or Spanish manuscripts require author-paid professional translation to English. Use the mandatory template; 10–20 pages including references are recommended, with titles/abstracts/keywords in Portuguese, English and Spanish. At most five authors and two submissions per author per year; no parallel manuscript in the journal. Doctoral authors undertake to join the reviewer pool. Follow the linked AI disclosure policy. No mandatory payment identified for English submissions under the journal’s no-fee policy. Paid professional translation applies to Portuguese or Spanish submissions; doctoral authors undertake to join reviewer pool if accepted.

**AI policy:** Journal policy explicitly covers AI use in literature review, analysis, text generation and modeling with human scientific responsibility.

**Disclosure (required):** Describe substantive use in the manuscript including tool name, version and purpose.
