# Economics, econometrics and research economics

Area: Economics. Checked November 16, 2026. These are venue comparisons, not an acceptance prediction. [Methodology](../methodology.md).

## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### EKOIST Journal of Econometrics and Statistics

**Difficulty:** moderate (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within applied econometrics/statistics: the official 2025 outcome table reports 36 accepted and 35 rejected articles, rounded to 51% acceptance. Together with an editor’s originality/methodology/significance screen and at least two referees, this supports a moderate rather than an easy label for well-matched quantitative economic work. The outcome table does not estimate an individual manuscript’s odds.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. University-funded publication: no charge to authors or institutions under any name, including submission, processing and publication. English or Turkish manuscripts; author instructions specify 4,500–8,500 words. No affiliation, membership or conference condition accompanies the fee policy.

**Fit:** Quantitative economics and applications of econometric/statistical methods to measurement, prediction, testing and policy analysis. Economic content is emphasized; reusable and replicable methods fit. English and Turkish accepted.

[Author guidance](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ekoist/writing-rules) · [Venue](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ekoist)

- [Fee evidence](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ekoist/price-policy) (2026-11-16, page): Istanbul University covers all expenses; no fee is charged to authors or institutions under any name, and submission/processing/publication are expressly free.
- [Fee evidence](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ekoist/writing-rules) (2026-11-16, page): Instructions specify a 4,500–8,500-word manuscript and do not list paid membership, page or registration requirements.
- [Acceptance ratio (%)](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/ekoist/rejection-statistics): 51; year/period: 2025; population: 2025 table: 36 accepted and 35 rejected articles, implying 71 tabulated accept/reject outcomes. Withdrawn, returned, pending manuscripts and submission-year cohort rules are not defined as part of this denominator.. Official rounded percentage, not recomputed precision: 36/(36+35) is approximately 50.70%. Use only the EKOIST table; the separate predecessor-title rows of zeros are not this journal’s acceptance rate. The unselected time-statistics panel was not assigned a guessed year.

### Central European Economic Journal

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative difficulty band established. Official scope requires original theoretical/empirical research or reviews that advance the field, with findings relevant beyond a single country and clear international-literature contributions where applicable. These are substantive fit requirements but do not disclose a comparative selectivity level; Central/Eastern European relevance does not imply easy acceptance.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Current journal-specific instructions exclude submission and article-processing fees, without a waiver, institutional affiliation or paid membership condition. English manuscripts normally must not exceed 40 A4 pages. From November 2026, manuscript declarations must state whether and how generative AI was used; AI tools cannot be authors and humans retain responsibility.

**Fit:** Theoretical and empirical economics/finance and interdisciplinary research; explicit areas include policy evaluation, econometrics, machine learning, population and household well-being. Central/Eastern Europe is emphasized; other-country work needs an international contribution. Systematic or critical reviews are accepted; opinion papers are confined to special issues.

[Author guidance](https://reference-global.com/download/document/journal/CEEJ/Submission_and_Formatting_Instructions.pdf) · [Venue](https://reference-global.com/journal/CEEJ)

- [Fee evidence](https://reference-global.com/download/document/journal/CEEJ/Submission_and_Formatting_Instructions.pdf) (2026-11-16, page): Official instructions explicitly exclude submission and article-processing fees, specify the 40-page norm, and introduce generative-AI declarations for articles published from November 2026.
- Difficulty evidence: [source 1](https://reference-global.com/journal/CEEJ), [source 2](https://reference-global.com/download/document/journal/CEEJ/Submission_and_Formatting_Instructions.pdf)

### Comparative Economic Studies

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative difficulty band established. The official scope requires original political-economy analysis and a comparative perspective while allowing single-country/region cases, but discloses no acceptance statistic or specific selectivity threshold sufficient to stratify difficulty. Citation impact and rapid first decisions do not supply that missing evidence.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Use the standard subscription route, for which no APC applies; journal-specific instructions also exclude submission charges. Decline paid print colour by supplying greyscale figures or explicitly accepting greyscale reproduction. Immediate OA, institutional OA funding and offprints are separate optional choices. Immediate-OA funder obligations may make the subscription licence unsuitable for a particular manuscript.

**Fit:** Comparative political economy and economic systems; international or single-country studies of institutions, technology, demography, structural change and policy. Existing-data and theoretical institutional analyses can fit.

### Economía (PUCP)

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative difficulty band established. Official search-indexed policies describe originality/relevance screening and double-blind external review, but a current year-defined selectivity statistic was not verified. Latin American emphasis and open access supply no basis to infer easy acceptance.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Official About policy broadly excludes fees to authors and readers and says institutional resources fully support publication; no selective waiver or author affiliation/membership condition is attached. Full author-guideline retrieval remained restricted. The full official AI policy was retrieved: supportive AI use must be disclosed, authorship and responsibility remain human, and AI-generated/manipulated images are prohibited.

**Fit:** Original theoretical and empirical research across economics, with an emphasis on Latin America and an international author community. Existing-data economic studies and economically substantive methods can fit. Research-data guidance encourages sharing supporting data, software, code and models; this is not a standalone data/software journal.

### Erasmus Journal for Philosophy and Economics

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative difficulty band established. Research articles require editorial assessment of relevance, coherence, scholarship and significance, followed by at least two expert double-blind reviews. Support for young scholars and a constructive process does not establish easy acceptance; book reviews, interviews and thesis summaries have distinct review status.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. No submission fee, APC, or other publication fee; journal pays accepted-article copy-editing. Academic articles normally have 4,000–8,000 words including references; exceptional longer articles may be considered without a listed overlength fee. AI use beyond grammar/readability editing requires disclosure, and substantially AI-written arguments or prose are not considered.

**Fit:** Methodology, history, ethics and interdisciplinary relations of economics; critical surveys and arguments on incentives or research institutions may fit. Research articles are peer reviewed; book reviews, interviews and thesis summaries have different editorial status. Assess manuscript-level compatibility with the journal's AI policy.

### Journal of Mechanism and Institution Design

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Reviewer judgment: no comparative difficulty band is established. Official policy requires originality, validity and significance and permits desk rejection for insufficient priority, but supplies neither acceptance statistics nor a specific contribution threshold that calibrates high difficulty relative to other economics journals. Replicable data and substantive mechanism/institution design remain fit requirements; unknown does not imply easy acceptance.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Community-sponsored journal excludes manuscript-processing and publication fees; sponsor donations cover all publication charges. Author guide imposes no manuscript page limit. Society conferences are separate events and are not prerequisites for ordinary journal submission.

**Fit:** Mechanism and institution design across economic, financial, political and social systems. Theory, empirical analysis and computation are eligible; results must be replicable and underlying data available to the journal. Suitable for incentives/allocation studies with a substantive design contribution.

### Swiss Journal of Economics and Statistics

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative difficulty band established. The official journal welcomes contributions across economics and early-career researchers, but neither that welcome nor an efficient review target establishes acceptance probability. Pure statistics is out of scope; no year-defined selectivity statistic was located.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The owning Swiss Society of Economics and Statistics explicitly makes submission free for interested researchers worldwide. Springer’s journal-specific policy states that publication is free because the society covers the APC. This is journal-wide sponsorship, not a selective author waiver or an affiliation agreement. No author membership, page surcharge or conference registration requirement was found.

**Fit:** All fields of economics, including theoretical, empirical and policy work. Despite the title, pure statistics is explicitly excluded. Existing-data economic studies fit; statistical methods need an economic contribution.
