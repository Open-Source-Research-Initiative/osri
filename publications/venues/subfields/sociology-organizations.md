### Acta Sociologica

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative band established. Long articles must make a significant original sociological contribution; short articles may make narrower relevant contributions. Statistical/computational research requires a replication package subject to legal/ethical limits. The official 12.8% acceptance display has no named period or defined cohort, so it cannot calibrate comparative acceptance difficulty. Nordic association or regional scope does not imply easy acceptance.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Use subscription publication; avoid optional paid OA/print color. Long articles: 5,000–10,000 words; short articles: at most 5,000. Limits are strict. Generic page-charge wording has no actual schedule. Preprints accepted without updates during review. Disclose generative AI; prompt-generated submissions/data, synthetic interviews, and AI analysis of experiences/themes are prohibited. Independent authors permitted; extra integrity documentation may be requested.

**Fit:** General sociology including quantitative and computational existing-data research. Statistical/computational papers require a public replication package subject to ethical and legal restrictions. Comments receive editorial review only and are not represented here as peer-reviewed original research.

### Journal of World-Systems Research

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative band established. Official policy screens for content, quality and form, seeks three external expert reviews, and emphasizes explicit concepts, hypotheses, theory construction and causal/comparative analysis relevant to world-systems. This establishes substantial scholarly and scope requirements but no comparative selectivity threshold or year-defined acceptance rate. Specialist scope and diamond access do not establish easier acceptance.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Regular research articles use English and normally 8,000–10,000 words; longer work requires cover-letter justification. Book reviews are solicited, with queries welcomed. Authors must secure rights to third-party material; avoid material that requires paid permission to retain a zero-cost workflow. Prepublication manuscripts may be posted during submission.

**Fit:** Comparative, historical, quantitative and qualitative research relevant to world-systems. Existing international, organizational or network data can support causal and comparative work when the world-systems connection is explicit. Associated datasets are welcomed with articles; this does not establish a standalone data-paper route. Generic organizational studies without world-systems relevance are a weak fit.

### Przegląd Socjologiczny

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). No comparative band established. Two independent reviewers assess originality, topic significance, contribution, methods, argument and presentation; a third may resolve disagreement and editors decide. National and international sociology are eligible, with a sociological perspective required for related disciplines. These establish quality/fit requirements, but neither regional focus nor absent retrieved metrics establish easy acceptance.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Online-only submission since June 16, 2026. About 40,000 characters; maximum 50,000 including spaces/bibliography and excluding abstracts. English metadata required; Polish authors also supply Polish metadata. Supply identified and anonymized editable files. Disclose AI use with significant impact on content/form; authors remain responsible and AI cannot be an author. Preprints are accepted: disclose and link at submission, and update with the published DOI where possible. Affiliation does not determine editorial evaluation.

**Fit:** Polish and international sociology across social research and theory; related disciplines require a sociological perspective. Existing-data studies of institutions, organizations and collaboration or theoretical syntheses may fit as sociological articles. Polish and English accepted; English contributions may be grouped in the English-language issue. Book reviews, polemics and debates are separate formats.
