## Current Sociology

**Fit:** International sociology across substantive research, theory, concepts, and methods; explicitly solicits reviews of emerging substantive, conceptual, theoretical, and methodological controversies. Existing-data and synthesis work needs a sociological contribution of international interest.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Use subscription publication within 8,000 words; avoid optional paid OA/print color. Preprint status conflicts internally: key information says no, detailed policy says yes. Resolve before posting or submitting an existing preprint. Disclose generative AI; prompt-generated submissions/data, synthetic interviews, and AI analysis of experiences/themes are prohibited. Independent authors permitted; extra integrity documentation may be requested. None stated for the compliant subscription route. Paid OA, requested print color and third-party permissions are separate choices.

**AI policy:** Sage identifies literature assistance and generated or checked code for research as appropriate with disclosure and verification. Its discussion of primary or partial generated text, its statement that disclosed GenAI alone is not grounds for rejection, and non-exhaustive appropriate-use examples together support conditional human-led partial drafting by inference; the prompt-generated submission boundary remains ambiguous. Authors retain intellectual responsibility. Current title-page access failed; official older instructions refer to Sage AI policy but cannot rule out later title-specific restrictions.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose generated content affecting methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission; report appropriate research uses in methods or acknowledgements. Suggestions for language, grammar or structure alone do not require disclosure under Sage policy.

## Organization

**Fit:** Critical and theory-driven studies of organizations and organizing. Existing-data analyses and Critical Methodologies papers can fit if they engage the critical management conversation; a generic computational performance paper would not satisfy that scope.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Use subscription publication; avoid optional paid OA/print color. Standard papers: 11,000 words excluding references; Critical Methodologies: 8,000 excluding references. Book/media reviews have review exceptions. Preprints accepted without updates during review. Disclose generative AI; prompt-generated submissions/data, synthetic interviews, and AI analysis of experiences/themes are prohibited. Independent authors permitted; extra integrity documentation may be requested. None stated for the compliant subscription route. Paid OA, requested print color and third-party permissions are separate choices.

**AI policy:** Sage identifies literature assistance, generated research code and code checking as appropriate when disclosed and verified. Its current policy contemplates primary or partial generated text impacting research, says disclosed GenAI use alone does not cause rejection, and presents appropriate uses as examples. Together these support conditional human-led partial drafting by inference; the policy does not expressly authorize paragraphs or define the boundary with a submission generated through prompts. Authors retain responsibility for accuracy and original sources.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose primary or partial generated content impacting methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission; the listed appropriate research uses require disclosure in methods or acknowledgements. Routine suggestions to improve authors’ own language, grammar or structure do not require disclosure. Verify outputs and original sources.

## Rationality and Society

**Fit:** Social theory and research grounded in or critically engaging rational action, including cooperation, collective action, organizational behavior, and formal models. Qualitative, quantitative, theoretical and empirical work are welcome; the rational-action connection is a scope constraint. Empirical papers require replication information subject to legal and ethical restrictions.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Use subscription publication; avoid optional paid OA/print color. No formal word limit; 8,000–10,000 words is typical. Research articles are peer reviewed; editorials are an exception. Preprints accepted without updates during review. Disclose generative AI; prompt-generated submissions/data, synthetic interviews, and AI analysis of experiences/themes are prohibited. Independent authors permitted; extra integrity documentation may be requested. None stated for the compliant subscription route. Paid OA, requested print color and third-party permissions are separate choices.

**AI policy:** Sage expressly lists literature-review support and generated or checked research code as appropriate, subject to disclosure and human verification. Conditional human-led partial drafting is inferred from its treatment of primary or partial generated text, statement that disclosed use alone is not grounds for rejection, and non-exhaustive examples; it is not an explicit permission for drafting paragraphs. The boundary with its ban on generating a submission through a series of prompts is undefined.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose primary or partial generated content affecting methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission; Sage gives methods or acknowledgements as places for specified research uses. Language, grammar and structure suggestions improving an author’s own work need no disclosure. Verify output and cite original sources.
