## Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter

**Fit:** Theoretical and simulation studies in condensed matter, electronic structure, phase transitions, surfaces, soft matter and physics of materials. Papers must contain new fundamental condensed-matter science; routine property calculations without new physics do not meet the stated scope.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Choose subscription access; paid gold open access is optional. Papers are normally at most 8,500 words; letters have a six-page/4,500-word limit. Reviews are mainly commissioned, although submitted reviews can be considered. The subscription route does not require a waiver, institutional agreement or paid membership. The stated article-length rules are editorial limits, not a paid overlength option. None for standard subscription publication: this journal explicitly excludes all other publishing charges, including any separate page or colour charge.
