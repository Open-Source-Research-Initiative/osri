## Administration & Society

**Fit:** Theory-grounded empirical public administration research and work developing normative or positive theory. Existing-data studies and conceptual or methodological Perspectives can fit; follow the different research/Perspectives length limits.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** The journal explicitly excludes submission and publication fees for its subscription route. Sage Choice OA is optional and paid; institutional OA coverage is conditional. Research manuscripts may not exceed 35 double-spaced pages excluding references/tables/charts/notes; Perspectives may not exceed 25 inclusive pages. Online colour is free; requested print colour is charged. No mandatory membership, paid registration or standard page fee is stated in the inspected journal-specific guide. Paid preparation services and third-party reuse permissions are outside the ordinary author route.

**AI policy:** Sage identifies literature assistance and generated or checked code for research as appropriate with disclosure and verification. Its discussion of primary or partial generated text, its statement that disclosed GenAI alone is not grounds for rejection, and non-exhaustive appropriate-use examples together support conditional human-led partial drafting by inference; the prompt-generated submission boundary remains ambiguous. Authors retain intellectual responsibility.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose generated content affecting methodology, analysis, results or conclusions upon submission; report appropriate research uses in methods or acknowledgements. Suggestions for language, grammar or structure alone do not require disclosure under Sage policy.
