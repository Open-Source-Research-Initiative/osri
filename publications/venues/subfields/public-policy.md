# Public policy, governance and administration

Area: Public Policy. Checked November 16, 2026. These are venue comparisons, not an acceptance prediction. [Methodology](../methodology.md).

## Verified free peer-reviewed routes

### Public Policy and Administration

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within public-policy venues: the official aggregate acceptance rate is 7.3%; editors screen scope and quality before double-anonymized review, and two independent reviews are required for revision/acceptance. Original research must contribute to public policy/administration. The aggregate covers all article types and is not a manuscript-specific acceptance probability.

**Route:** subscription; subscription_with_optional_open_access. The journal-specific guide explicitly excludes submission and publication fees. Standard subscription publication is the verified free route; paid Sage Choice OA and institutional OA agreements are optional. Maximum manuscript length is 8,000 words. Online colour is free; author-requested print colour is charged. The guide also contains generic wording that OA fees exclude page/colour charges. Sage’s current publisher policy lists active journals with submission, page or publication charges; this journal is absent, and its guide supplies no page-price or charge trigger. This supports the stated standard route; it does not establish a free overlength allowance or cover optional production requests.

**Fit:** Public policy, government, public management, administrative theory, history and politics. Existing-data and comparative policy analyses can fit when they make an original contribution. The current limit is 8,000 words.

### Science, Technology, & Human Values

**Difficulty:** very high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment for STS/governance work in this public-policy cluster: the official aggregate acceptance rate is 9.2%. The author guide requires original in-scope work and allows desk rejection for insufficient quality or fit before double-anonymized review. The rate supports a demanding venue-level band, without making citation impact a difficulty proxy or implying an individual manuscript's odds.

**Route:** subscription; subscription_with_optional_open_access. The journal explicitly excludes submission and publication fees for its subscription route. Sage Choice OA is optional and paid; institutional OA coverage is conditional. Original articles may contain up to 8,000 words excluding abstract/endnotes/references; review essays have a 5,000-word limit. Colour reproduction is expressly free in both online and print editions.

**Fit:** Science and technology studies, including relationships to governance, politics and society. Existing documentary/data research or conceptual analysis needs a substantive STS contribution. A generic technology application paper is insufficient.

### Administration & Society

Research must be grounded in relevant theory or develop/extend normative or positive theory; editors can decline unsuitable work before review. ### Journal of Deliberative Democracy

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment within deliberative-democracy research: the official review policy explicitly states that annual article capacity is limited and asks reviewers to be selective. Editors screen suitability before at least two experts assess theoretical/methodological/empirical rigour, engagement with debates, and implications for theory/practice. Research papers must make an original contribution. No official acceptance rate was located; the band does not infer ease or odds from diamond funding or citation quartile.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The journal says sponsors and University of Westminster Press cover APCs; authors pay no publication fee. The editors' July 2026 official Digest statement excludes author fees at every stage. Institutional/library support is encouraged, not required. Research articles are 6,000–8,000 words inclusive; Reflections are 2,000–4,000. Special Features are solicited under author guidelines, while the submissions table also marks that section publicly open: verify that format before proposing it. Special issues/book reviews are no longer considered. Authors obtain third-party permissions.

**Fit:** Deliberative and participatory democracy, citizen engagement and democratic governance. Existing-data studies, comparative analysis and theory can fit when they advance deliberative-democracy scholarship. Research articles and Reflections from the Field receive peer review; the associated Digest is a separate non-peer-reviewed publication.

### Internet Policy Review

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The author guide and desk criteria require European/pan-European relevance, original analytical scholarship and transparent evidence; purely descriptive work generally does not proceed. This establishes fit and a review threshold, but no official acceptance rate or comparative selectivity evidence sufficient for a difficulty band was found. The October 2026 backlog notice concerns screening time rather than acceptance probability.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The official author policy excludes submission fees and APCs. Research submissions remain invited; the 5 October 2026 notice warns of delayed desk screening. Op-ed proposals are closed through 2026. Initial research papers should be about 6,000 words; final papers at most 8,000, excluding abstract/references. Human authors retain intellectual accountability. The webpage’s short prohibition on AI authoring articles coexists with the linked September 2026 policy: language/readability editing needs no disclosure if substantive content originates with authors; generated, drafted or substantially restructured text requires acknowledgement naming the tool and use. AI visuals must be labelled and cannot represent empirical data or simulate results. Editors may seek clarification; undisclosed integrity-affecting use may lead to rejection/retraction.

**Fit:** Internet regulation, technology governance and socio-technical change. Empirical, legal, theoretical and documentary analysis can fit; research papers must engage European policy and contribute original critical analysis. This record covers externally peer-reviewed research papers.

### Policy Quarterly

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). The official ethics policy bases decisions on importance, originality, clarity and relevance; editors may reject out-of-scope or manifestly weak work before normally sending suitable articles to two experts. The accessible policy-writing remit and roughly 4,000-word format do not establish easier acceptance. No official acceptance statistics or comparative selectivity evidence sufficient for a difficulty band was located. Major speeches are an explicit editorial exception.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. The official ethics policy excludes manuscript-processing, submission and publication charges, without an affiliation or waiver condition. The submission page welcomes articles of about 4,000 words concerning governance, public policy and management. Ordinary submitted articles receive peer review; some major speeches are an editorial exception. No submission closure was present in the inspected pages.

**Fit:** Governance, public policy and public management, written for public-sector practitioners and other policy readers. Concise existing-data analysis, evaluations and policy arguments can fit; New Zealand relevance is prominent in published content. Submitted articles receive peer review, while occasional published major speeches can bypass it.
