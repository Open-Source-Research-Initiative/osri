## AI, Law, Politics

**Fit:** Political institutions, public policy, law and governance including conceptual and empirical work. AI need not be the subject.

**Submission fee:** none. **Publication fee:** none.

**Conditions and other costs:** Journal states publication is free for authors, including no submission, processing, publication, page, or colour fees. Manuscripts over stated length limits require prior editorial approval. No mandatory author costs stated; the journal explicitly says publication is free for authors. No paid membership or conference registration requirement identified.

**AI policy:** Journal permits disclosed AI-assisted text, code, data and analysis with human verification and full author responsibility. Manuscripts predominantly AI-generated without substantial human intellectual contribution are not accepted.

**Disclosure (required_except_editing):** Disclose tool, provider, model/version where available, access dates, purpose, stage and extent, and human verification. Initial full statement goes on title page; include anonymized statement if needed for review; accepted manuscript has Declarations before references. If AI is a research instrument or study object, describe settings, prompts, dates and validation in methodology. Routine non-generative spelling, formatting and reference management are exempt.
