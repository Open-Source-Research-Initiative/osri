**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment: official sources describe acceptance of approximately one quarter of submitted articles. The current general-information page reports 23.7%, whereas the 2024 report and EID Advantage describe about 25%; none specifies a comparable acceptance cohort. Emerging-infection/public-health relevance remains a separate fit condition.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. No author fees for ordinary articles, including submission, pages and corrections. Article-specific limits are enforced; research and synopsis articles normally allow 3500 text words. No required society membership or registration is stated for ordinary research/synopsis articles. Supplement organizers incur separate costs; this route does not cover organizing a supplement.

**Fit:** Epidemiology and prevention of emerging or reemerging infectious diseases worldwide. OSRI fit includes existing surveillance-data analyses, modeling and syntheses with an explicit public health contribution; laboratory and clinical portions of journal scope are outside this catalog intended initial research use.

The count and acceptance summary are journal-wide, not a forecast for an OSRI paper. European relevance is a scope condition; the editorial explicitly includes relevant work from outside Europe.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Ordinary journal articles use a diamond route free to authors and readers under ECDC funding. No waiver, institutional payment agreement or paid membership is needed for this stated route. The journal author pages remain blocked; check current article-type length and submission requirements before preparing a manuscript. This record does not establish supplement-organizer terms.

**Fit:** Communicable-disease epidemiology, surveillance, prevention and control with European relevance, including relevant work conducted outside Europe. ECDC expressly lists regular research, surveillance and outbreak articles, reviews, rapid communications and perspectives. Existing surveillance-data analyses and syntheses can fit. Exact current article-length and submission requirements still need journal-author-guidance confirmation because that page returned HTTP 403.

### Preventing Chronic Disease

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment: a CDC announcement explicitly reports 14.2% acceptance in 2025, while editorial policy prioritizes novel ideas for a broad public-health audience. The denominator and article mix are not disclosed. The two-year data recency requirement is a fit condition, not a prestige measure.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. Ordinary submission/publication has no journal fees under the blanket author-cost policy. Follow article-specific limits and the two-year data-recency policy. Tools for Public Health Practice requires an editor summary before submission and is an instructional methods format. CDC publication and reuse-permission policies apply. No paid membership or editing prerequisite is stated.

**Fit:** Chronic-disease prevention and population-health practice, including surveillance/survey and GIS analyses, evaluation and systematic reviews. Scoping reviews are excluded from the systematic-review category. The methods label maps to practical instructional Tools for Public Health Practice articles, which require a one-paragraph summary to the editor before manuscript submission; it does not establish a generic algorithm-paper format. Current guidance says submitted data should be the most current and no older than two years.

### Public Health Reports

**Difficulty:** high (provisional agent judgment). Provisional agent judgment based on historical selectivity: the editors report 15.2% acceptance among 656 final decisions in 2023. This is a mixed peer/editor-reviewed regular-issue population, not current research-article odds. No 2024/2025 acceptance report was found in the bounded search; US public-health relevance is a separate fit condition.

**Route:** subscription; subscription. Standard subscription publication is free to submit and publish. Online colour is free; requested print colour and optional OA incur separate charges. Follow article-specific limits rather than assuming a paid excess-length option: the hosted instructions normally set 3000 words for original research and methodology. Some brief reports/commentaries require prior editorial agreement. No mandatory society membership is stated.

**Fit:** Public-health practice, epidemiologic research, evaluation, methodology, meta-analysis and topical synthesis with practical implications. The methodology category asks for applications and examples using original data; a purely conceptual method description is not an established fit. For work involving human participants or human data, guidance requires an institutional IRB, nonresearch or exemption basis; authors should not independently declare themselves exempt. Original research, methodology and topical reviews are peer reviewed; some other categories are editor assessed or need prior agreement. Recent data, preferably under five years old, are favored.

### Bulletin of the World Health Organization

**Difficulty:** unknown (provisional agent judgment). Initial editorial screening requires originality, scientific rigour and explicit new knowledge, but no acceptance statistic or comparative contribution threshold was found. These requirements alone do not establish a relative difficulty band. International relevance and country-affiliation rules are fit conditions, not a prestige score.

**Route:** diamond; open_access. WHO charges no author fees. Research, systematic reviews and policy/practice articles normally have 3000-word limits; there is no paid excess-length route stated. Abstract translation is free. Supplementary material for accepted work must be placed in a DOI-issuing repository; use a free suitable repository. Authors obtain permissions for copyrighted reuse. Apply current affiliation, review-search recency and preprint restrictions.
Fully open access with no author fees. Research, systematic reviews and Policy & practice normally allow 3,000 words; follow format-specific limits. English submissions; translated abstracts cost authors nothing. Guidelines restrict prior publication, with a preprint exception only for research in a public health emergency of international concern. Primary research requires study-country institutional representation.

**Fit:** International public-health research, systematic reviews and policy analysis; local findings need international significance. For primary research, at least one author must have an institutional affiliation in the country where the research took place. Systematic reviews require multilingual searches performed within six months of submission; guidance also calls for protocol registration. The journal normally excludes work published elsewhere, with a preprint exception specifically for research in a public-health emergency of international concern. These conditions materially limit independent-author and ordinary preprinted-work fit.
