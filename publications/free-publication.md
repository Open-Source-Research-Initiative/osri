# Choose a free publication route

OSRI prioritizes research submitted to credible venues that offer free submission and a free publication route. People choose research questions, direct Agentic-Powered-Research, check the evidence, and decide when a manuscript is ready for a suitable venue.

Use the [venue directory](venues/README.md) to compare options within your subfield. It retains fee conditions, editorial difficulty judgments, and available official metrics. These are candidate venues for authors to assess; a directory entry does not select a target for a particular project.

## Build a shortlist

Choose venues that accept your subject, study type, and contribution. Begin with the verified free peer-reviewed list. Read the conditions and current author instructions, including restrictions on AI-assisted work and preprints. A journal's reputation does not establish that a particular manuscript fits.

Check both submission and publication costs. Include page limits, mandatory membership, and required conference registration. Record optional paid choices so they are not selected by mistake. Conditional waivers or institutional funding may help an eligible author, but they stay separate from a generally free route. Unknown costs stay unverified.

Prefer diamond open access when the work fits. It provides access without author or reader charges. A subscription journal may offer a free author route while restricting reader access; check any permitted preprint or accepted-manuscript sharing route separately.

## Compare editorial demands

Difficulty bands are provisional agent judgments based on editorial criteria or reported selectivity within a subfield. Moderate does not mean easy. Missing evidence remains unknown. Citation indicators and published acceptance rates retain their years, definitions, and sources; they do not predict a manuscript's acceptance.

Compare the contribution a venue asks for with what the study establishes. A technically sound replication, review, method, software package, or substantive new result may suit different venues. Read [the directory methodology](venues/methodology.md) before treating its rankings as a submission plan.

## Record the human decision

Record the chosen article type, free route, dated fee sources, conditions, and the human lead's target decision in the project. A published target comparison remains distinct from submission, acceptance, and publication. Continue with [Publish and record your research](publish.md) for human review and authorization of the version to submit.
