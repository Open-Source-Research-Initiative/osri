## LinkedIn launch post

Choosing where to submit a paper gets harder when publication fees are out of reach. I'm building OSRI's venue directory to make the options easier to inspect.

It separates submission and publication costs, records scope and eligibility conditions, links AI disclosure instructions, and shows the evidence behind its editorial and indexing checks. You can compare up to three venues and share the shortlist URL.

A free author route can still restrict reader access. AI use remains subject to the venue's rules and human responsibility. The directory helps narrow a search; the current official author instructions still matter.

If you work in public health, health services, education or behavioral research, I'd value a 10-minute trial on one topic. Did you find a plausible venue? What was missing or confusing?

Try it: https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/

The walkthrough and feedback links are on the page. There are also six small contributor tasks for anyone who would like to check a source or improve a comparison.

## OSRI announcement draft

Title: Try the venue directory and help check its sources

OSRI's venue directory helps researchers compare routes with free submission and a free publication option. It keeps author costs, scope, author eligibility, AI disclosure and editorial evidence together, with links to the underlying sources.

Try it on one public research topic, compare up to three venues, and share the URL to keep the shortlist. We welcome a plausible fit or a clear reason nothing fits.

- Directory: https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/
- Short walkthrough: https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/guides/docs/venue-directory-demo/
- Report your experience: https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory/issues/new?template=directory-use.yml
- Six starter tasks: https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/guides/community/venue-starter-tasks/

A browser and a short note are enough to contribute. Source checks include Research Ethics author costs, JOSS AI disclosure, editorial leadership and article traceability. Cole / @ajhcs reviews acceptance; maintainers prepare data changes. Material agent assistance is disclosed, and public credit is optional.

Help us evaluate which comparisons are useful and which sources need another check. Feedback and source notes are reviewed before directory changes are accepted.

Source: https://github.com/Open-Source-Research-Initiative/free-publication-directory

## Use and follow-through

Use the direct field link appropriate to each recipient; add a personal sentence only when it is true. Keep the first five invitations individual. Log responses locally in [pilot-log.template.csv](pilot-log.template.csv), including no-fit results and declines. Ask before quoting anyone or turning email into a public record. Send at most one follow-up and respect requests for no further contact.

After an accepted first contribution, offer one relevant second task based on the person's interests. Do not promise authorship, a maintainer role, publication or a response schedule we cannot maintain.
