# First task: independently check Research Ethics submission and publication costs

## Goal

Check the author-cost record for **Research Ethics**, keeping submission and publication costs separate.

**For:** someone comfortable reading author instructions. **First attempt:** 15 minutes.

## Start

Read the [official submission guidelines](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/rea), especially publishing fees. Confirm the journal identity before following publisher-wide links. You do not need to contact the journal or make a submission.

## Return

- Submission cost and standard publication cost as two separate findings.
- Applicable article types and any compulsory page, membership, registration or other charges.
- Whether an option is mandatory, optional open access, or conditional on funding/waiver eligibility. Reader subscription costs are a separate matter.
- Official URLs, relevant section names, date checked, and anything the evidence does not establish. A short paraphrase is enough; do not copy entire pages.

## Acceptance

A source-backed note another reader can reproduce. Confirmation of the current record is useful. Silence about a charge does not prove zero cost. Maintainers independently review the finding and add dated follow-up evidence without rewriting the historical cost campaign.

Human maintainer: **Cole / @ajhcs**. Comment that you would like to help, and wait for a maintainer to confirm the claim before starting a substantial check. Claim one task at a time; the launch starts with at most three active claims across these issues. The timebox is a suggested first attempt. If a source is blocked or unclear, report that limit.

Reply here with a short note and source links. **No code, installation, fork or pull request is required.** Include the date checked and any material AI/agent assistance. Please use only information you may share publicly; a public research-topic description is enough.
