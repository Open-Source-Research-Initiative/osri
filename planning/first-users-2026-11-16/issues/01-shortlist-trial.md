# First task: try a publication shortlist for one research topic

## Goal

Help us find out whether the directory helps a researcher choose plausible publication venues. We want honest outcomes, including “none fits.”

**For:** a researcher with a current or planned paper. **First attempt:** 10–15 minutes.

## Start

Open the [venue directory](https://open-source-research-initiative.github.io/venues/). Select your field, read the scope and conditions, and compare up to three venues. Use a public topic or a description you are comfortable sharing; do not upload a manuscript.

## Return

- Research field and article type, with a brief public topic description.
- A shortlist/comparison URL, or the filters used if nothing fits.
- One plausible venue and why it fits, or why none does. Note relevant cost, scope, author-eligibility or AI-disclosure conditions.
- One confusing step, missing fact or helpful detail; say where you needed an explanation.

## Acceptance

An actual use report with an actionable observation. A visit or “looks good” alone is insufficient; a well-explained no-fit result is useful. This issue can host more than one pilot report if maintainers agree distinct tasks with participants. We will ask separately before quoting your experience publicly.
