# First task: verify JOSS AI-use and disclosure instructions

## Goal

Check what **Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS)** permits and what authors must disclose. Preserve the distinction between research, analysis/code, drafting and language editing.

**For:** a researcher or research-software contributor. **First attempt:** 20 minutes.

## Start

Read the [official JOSS submission guidance](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html), including its AI policy and any directly linked instructions.

## Return

- The AI uses explicitly addressed and the restrictions attached to them.
- What authors must disclose, where the disclosure goes, and whether different instructions apply to software and the paper.
- Exact official section links and date checked.
- Any permission that remains unclear. Do not generalize language-editing permission into permission for substantive research or drafting.

## Acceptance

A concise policy note whose interpretation can be checked from the linked text. This task reviews instructions; it does not scientifically review a software paper or establish unrestricted AI permission.

## How to take this task

Human maintainer: **Cole / @ajhcs**. Comment that you would like to help, and wait for a maintainer to confirm the claim before starting a substantial check. Claim one task at a time; the launch starts with at most three active claims across these issues. The timebox is a suggested first attempt. If a source is blocked or unclear, report that limit.

Reply here with a short note and source links. **No code, installation, fork or pull request is required.** Include the date checked and any material AI/agent assistance. Please use only information you may share publicly; a public research-topic description is enough. For an email route without a GitHub account, contact [Cole](mailto:cole.lyons@pennmedicine.upenn.edu?subject=OSRI%20venue%20directory%20task).

Cole reviews the evidence and acceptance. Maintainers prepare any data changes and dated follow-up records; a comment does not automatically change a venue's status. Useful confirmation of an existing record can count. Public credit is optional: say which handle/name you prefer, or request no public credit.
