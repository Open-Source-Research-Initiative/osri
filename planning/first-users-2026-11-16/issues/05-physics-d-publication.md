# First task: trace one Journal of Physics D research article

## Goal

Document a traceable publication sample for **Journal of Physics D: Applied Physics**. Its current directory assessment has an unresolved publication-sample check.

**For:** someone comfortable locating an article and DOI. **First attempt:** 15–20 minutes.

## Start

Open the [official journal information](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/journal-of-physics-d-applied-physics/) and follow the journal homepage or contents links. If the publisher page cannot be read, an exact-journal Crossref metadata record is an alternative; identify that limitation.

## Return

- One recent research article's title, DOI and journal identity.
- Publication date if available, plus a publisher landing page or exact Crossref record.
- Whether the check used a publisher page or metadata only; report blocked pages accurately.

## Acceptance

A reproducible bibliographic match to this journal. DOI metadata establishes traceability within its limits; it does not establish scientific validity, the quality of the article, or how peer review actually occurred.
